    Checkpoint 64. Modeling with Systems of Differential Equations.

    Consider the system
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{x}' = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -2\\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}. \end{equation*}
    1. Identify the coefficient matrix.
      🔗
    2. How many dependent variables are present?
      🔗
    3. Determine the trace and determinant.
      🔗
    4. Write the characteristic polynomial.
      🔗
    5. Without solving the system completely, predict whether the equilibrium will be stable or unstable.
      🔗
    6. Give one real-world application that could be modeled by a system of differential equations.
      🔗
    🔗

    1. Find the characteristic polynomial.

    2. Determine the eigenvalues.
      🔗
    3. Find an eigenvector corresponding to each eigenvalue.

    4. Classify the equilibrium point.
      🔗
    5. Determine whether the origin is stable or unstable.

    For each solution below:

    1. Identify the mistake.

    Problem A: Partial Fractions
    🔗
    A student writes \(\frac{1}{s(s+1)} = \frac{1}{s} + \frac{1}{s+1}\) and concludes
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathcal{L}^{-1} \left\{ \frac{1}{s(s+1)} \right\} = 1+e^{-t}. \end{equation*}
    Problem B: Shifting Theorem
    🔗
    A student claims \(\mathcal{L}^{-1} \left\{ \frac{e^{-3s}}{s^2} \right\} = t-3.\)
    🔗
    Problem C: Complex Eigenvalues
    🔗
    For the system
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{x}' = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -3\\ 3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}, \end{equation*}
    a student finds \(\lambda=2\pm3i\) and concludes:
    🔗
    "The origin is a center."
    🔗
    Problem D: Initial Value Problems
    🔗
    A student is solving \(y'+2y=0, \qquad y(0)=3\) and writes
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathcal{L}\{y'\} = sY. \end{equation*}
    The student continues:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} sY+2Y=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} Y=0 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} y(t)=0. \end{equation*}
    🔗
